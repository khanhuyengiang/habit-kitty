# Change log

Newest first. The app bundles this file: it shows the latest entry once after an update, and the whole list from the Change log link beside the version in Settings. Versions before 0.7 are not kept here.

## 0.7.0 - 2026-11-26

### New
- **A new home menu: four note windows.** The home page is now **Do**, **Track**, **Keep** and **Studio**, each a big Kitty Library note window holding its mini-apps: Habit Kitty and Litterbox, Journal (with Clockwork and Bank to come), Mart, Library and Pawchievement, Gallery and Wardrobe. A number on a key says how much needs you now (habits to do, chores due, the diary page to write, lists to shop), and a card's title adds up its keys. The key of the app you used last glows with a wing on each side, and a white kitty naps on Habit Kitty's key.
- **Settings: Mini-apps in four note windows.** Each group of the home menu is a Kitty Library note window with a key for each mini-app that has its own settings, in the home menu's order.
- **Settings: Apps to show.** Choose which apps have a key on the home page (a setting of this device). Wardrobe is off until you switch it on here, and its own settings button is gone. A card with nothing left to show disappears.
- **Settings: Change log is a link.** It sits beside the version at the bottom (`v0.7.0 · Change log`) and opens the list in a pop-up.

### Changed
- **Names.** The home page is titled **Itty Bitty Kitty**, and the mini-apps drop "Kitty" from their names (Journal, Library, Mart, Gallery, Litterbox, Wardrobe). Habit Kitty keeps its name. **Kitty Achievement is now Pawchievement.**
- **Settings sections renamed:** Look is now **Appearance**, and Your day is now **Time**.
- **The change log starts at 0.7.** Older entries were removed.
- **Kitty Wardrobe: the animation sprites and skins** were redrawn to go together, and the room widget on the Android home screen was resized.
