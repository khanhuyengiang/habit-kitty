    Sound

    Kitties indoors (and strays outdoors) meow now and then, and may purr or meow when you pet them. Slide a sound all the way down to turn it off, or mute them all at once.

    Your day

    Choose when your day starts and ends. Feeding, stray kitties, treats, streaks and diary pages all count toward the day you are in, so a night owl can let the day run past midnight.

    Backup

    One .kitty file holds both apps: your kitties, every diary page and photo, and your Kitty Achievement quests and achievements (photos are scaled down so the file stays small). Save it on this device, or share it to Drive or by mail.

    Kitty alarms

      Game options
      Fix affection
      Works out your affection again from the heatmaps: every meal, missed day, sitter day, stray and treat. Handy if it looks off.
      How to use
      • Each cat stands for one habit. Do the habit, then feed the cat once a day: +5 affection.
      • A day runs from 2am to 2am.
      • Every missed day costs −15. After 3 missed days in a row a classic cat dies; a light-hearted one runs away.
      • Going away? Leave a cat with the sitter for up to 7 days (−5 a day, once every 30 days). Bring it home in time or it stays there.
      • Below 0 affection, cats only trust you with one cat at a time. Feeding strays and doing treats earn it back.
      • Heatmap shows each cat's month (switch between combined and individual); the gold outline is today.
      • Treats are one-offs (up to 15 XP a day). Stray kitties are small habits you might not do every day, like cleaning your desk after working from home; they repeat and keep their history (up to 10 XP a day), and nothing bad happens if you skip a day. Feed a stray by doing its habit. A daily stray can be adopted as a kitty and keeps its history in teal. A kitty you release to the stray colony becomes a stray again.
      • The Room tab has two places: indoors for your kitties and the garden outside, where strays turn up now and then (a stray that is due today has an 80% chance every 6 hours, the others 30%; one you feed there may wander off again, 50%). Rename either one with the Rename button under it.
      • You can keep up to 9 kitties.
