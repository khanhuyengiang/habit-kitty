      How to use
      • Each cat stands for one habit. Do the habit, then feed the cat once a day: +5 affection.
      • A day runs from 2am to 2am.
      • Every missed day costs −15. After 3 missed days in a row a classic cat dies; a light-hearted one runs away.
      • Going away? Leave a cat with the sitter for up to 7 days (−5 a day, once every 30 days). Bring it home in time or it stays there.
      • Below 0 affection, cats only trust you with one cat at a time. Treats and kibbles earn it back.
      • Heatmap shows each cat's month (switch between combined and individual); the gold outline is today.
      • Kibbles are one-offs (up to 15 xp a day). Treats are small habits you might not do every day, like cleaning your desk after working from home; they repeat and keep their history (up to 10 xp a day). A daily treat can become a kitty and keeps its history in teal.
      • You can keep up to 9 kitties.
